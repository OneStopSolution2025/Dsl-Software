Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Data protection terms between Client (Controller) and DSL Solutions & Services Sdn Bhd (Processor)

Last Updated: December 2025

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Service Agreement, Master Agreement, AMC Agreement, Subscription Agreement, or any other contractual document (“Agreement”) between:

Client (“Data Controller”)
and
DSL Solutions & Services Sdn Bhd (RapidReportz) (“Data Processor”)
collectively the “Parties”.

1. Definitions

“Data Controller” – The party that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.

“Data Processor” – DSL Solutions & Services Sdn Bhd, which processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

“Personal Data” – Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

“Processing” – Any operation performed on Personal Data (collection, storage, access, deletion, etc.).

“Sub-processor” – Third party engaged by the Processor to process data on behalf of the Controller.

“Services” – The data hosting, AI automation, OCR, report generation, or other enterprise services provided by RapidReportz.

“Applicable Laws” – PDPA (Malaysia), GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and any other relevant data protection regulations.

2. Role of the Parties

2.1 Controller Responsibilities

The Controller:

  • Determines the purpose and lawful basis of data processing.
  • Ensures all Personal Data submitted to RapidReportz is collected lawfully.
  • Obtains all necessary consents from individuals.
  • Ensures uploaded documents do not violate any legal or contractual rights.
  • Provides instructions for processing.

2.2 Processor Responsibilities

The Processor:

  • Processes data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
  • Ensures data is handled securely and confidentially.
  • Does not sell, share, disclose, or re-purpose Personal Data.
  • Does not process Personal Data outside Malaysia unless instructed or required for technical operations.

3. Description of Processing

3.1 Nature & Purpose

RapidReportz processes Personal Data for:

  • OCR extraction.
  • AI-based automation.
  • Accident sketch processing.
  • Template generation.
  • Report building.
  • Data hosting (if included in the Agreement).
  • System security and maintenance.

3.2 Types of Data

Depending on the Controller’s usage, data may include:

  • Names, ID details (if contained in uploaded documents).
  • Contact information.
  • Document contents (photos, PDFs, reports, forms).
  • Metadata and report outputs.
  • Any other data provided by the Controller.

3.3 Data Subjects

May include:

  • Customers.
  • Employees.
  • Drivers / insured individuals.
  • Witnesses.
  • Third parties mentioned in reports.
  • Any individuals appearing in uploaded files.

4. Non-Retention of Data (Default)

Unless explicitly included in the Agreement:

  • The Processor does not retain uploaded documents.
  • All files are processed in real-time.
  • Files are deleted immediately after processing.
  • Data is not stored in logs, backups, or long-term repositories.

This DPA applies fully only if the Controller has subscribed to enterprise hosting or data retention services.

5. Data Retention (Enterprise Hosting Only)

If included in the Agreement:

  • Personal Data will be stored securely in Malaysia (or the nearest Google Cloud regional data center).
  • Retention period follows the Controller’s instructions.
  • Backups follow the Controller-approved schedule.
  • Data is deleted upon instruction or contract termination.

If no instruction is provided, data will be deleted within 30–90 days after contract termination.

6. Sub-Processors

6.1 Approved Sub-Processors

The Controller authorizes the Processor to use the following sub-processors:

  • Cloud Hosting
    Google Cloud Platform (GCP) – Malaysia (or nearest region).
  • AI / OCR Providers (as needed)
    Third-party OCR engines and AI inference providers.
  • Email/SMS gateways.
  • Monitoring tools.
  • Customer support systems.

A full list will be provided upon request.

6.2 Sub-Processor Obligations

The Processor ensures all sub-processors:

  • Sign written agreements with equivalent data protection obligations.
  • Use industry-standard security.
  • Process data only as required to deliver the Services.

7. International Transfers

If data is transferred outside Malaysia:

  • Transfers will follow Applicable Laws.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) will be used where required.
  • Adequate technical and organizational safeguards will be applied.

8. Security Measures

The Processor implements, at a minimum:

  • AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • SSL/TLS encryption in transit.
  • Access control & authentication.
  • Network firewalls & monitoring.
  • Employee confidentiality agreements.
  • SOC2/ISO-certified cloud infrastructure (via providers).
  • Periodic penetration testing.
  • Risk assessment & incident response procedures.

9. Confidentiality

  • Processor personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Access is limited to roles necessary to fulfil the Agreement.
  • Confidentiality continues after contract termination.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a breach involving Personal Data:

  • The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay.
  • Provide details of the incident, impact, and remediation steps.
  • Cooperate fully with investigations.
  • Assist the Controller with regulatory notifications (if required).

The Processor is not liable for breaches caused by:

  • Controller systems.
  • User negligence.
  • Third-party systems outside the Processor’s control.

11. Data Subject Rights Assistance

The Processor will assist the Controller in responding to:

  • Access requests.
  • Correction requests.
  • Erasure requests.
  • Objections.
  • Data portability requests.
  • Restriction of processing.

The Processor is not directly responsible for responding to data subjects unless legally required.

12. Instructions from Controller

The Processor will process Personal Data only based on:

  • Written instructions.
  • Contractual obligations.
  • System functionality required to fulfil the Services.

If the Processor believes an instruction violates law, it will notify the Controller.

13. Return or Deletion of Data

Upon contract termination:

  • All hosted data will be deleted or returned per the Controller’s instructions.
  • If no instruction is provided, deletion will occur within 30–90 days.
  • Backups will be purged during the normal backup cycle.

The Processor will confirm deletion upon request.

14. Liability

The Processor’s total liability under this DPA is limited to:

  • The amount paid to the Processor under the Agreement in the preceding 12 months.

The Processor is not liable for:

  • AI interpretation errors.
  • Controller misuse of data.
  • Illegal or unauthorized content uploaded by the Controller.
  • Indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

15. Audit Rights

The Controller may audit the Processor’s compliance:

Transforming reporting workflows from manual to intelligent through innovative automation technology.
