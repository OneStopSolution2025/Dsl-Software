Privacy Policy

How RapidReportz collects, uses, and protects your personal information

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RapidReportz, operated by DSL Solutions & Services Sdn Bhd (“Company”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses, discloses, protects, and manages your personal information when you use our website, mobile application, or related services (“Services”).

By using the Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

We comply with the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), and where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account details (name, email, phone number, company name, password).
  • Billing and payment details.
  • Documents you upload for OCR, AI extraction, mapping, or report generation.
  • Communication data (support requests, messages, feedback).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data (page views, interaction logs, feature usage).
  • Device information (IP address, device type, OS, browser).
  • Cookies and tracking technologies.
  • Approximate location based on IP.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information to:

  • Provide and improve the RapidReportz Services.
  • Perform OCR, AI data extraction, auto-report generation, and automation.
  • Communicate service updates, support responses, and system notices.
  • Process billing and manage accounts.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, misuse, or security risks.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Marketing communications are only sent with your consent.

4. AI Processing and Disclaimers

4.1 Use of AI and Automation

RapidReportz uses AI/ML technologies for:

  • OCR document scanning.
  • Automated data extraction.
  • Accident sketch interpretation.
  • Report formatting and generation.
  • Learning user formatting preferences.

4.2 Accuracy Limitation

AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies or omissions. You are responsible for reviewing and verifying all AI-generated outputs before relying on them.

4.3 AI Training

We may use anonymised and aggregated data to improve AI models. We do not use identifiable document data to train models unless explicitly agreed in writing with enterprise clients.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process your data based on:

  • Your consent.
  • Contractual necessity.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Our legitimate interest in providing secure, functional, and improved Services.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Default Users (No Data Storage)

Unless you have a signed data-hosting agreement with us:

  • We do not store uploaded documents.
  • Processing is performed in real-time.
  • Files are deleted immediately after processing.
  • No content is saved in databases, logs, or backups.

This is our default operating model.

6.2 Enterprise Clients (Under Contract Only)

For enterprise customers who sign an AMC, hosting agreement, or data management contract:

  • Retention periods follow the contractual terms.
  • Data may be stored securely in our systems within Malaysia.
  • Backups, archives, and deletion timelines follow the signed agreement.

6.3 Account & Billing Data

We may retain:

  • Account details.
  • Billing records.
  • Security logs.
  • Transaction history.

as required for operational or legal purposes.

7. Sharing of Information

7.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share information with:

  • Google Cloud (hosting in Malaysia or nearest available region).
  • AI and OCR service partners.
  • Payment processors.
  • Analytics providers.
  • Email and support platforms.

These providers operate under strict confidentiality and security obligations.

7.2 User Responsibility for Uploaded Data

You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the legal rights and permissions to upload documents. We are not liable for illegal, inaccurate, or unauthorized content uploaded to the platform.

7.3 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose information to comply with:

  • Laws and regulations.
  • Court orders.
  • Government or enforcement requests.
  • To protect our legal rights.

7.4 Business Transfers

Your data may transfer in the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale.

8. International Transfers

Data is stored primarily in Malaysia (or the nearest available Google Cloud region such as Singapore). Where data is transferred outside Malaysia, we implement appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

9. Security Measures

We implement:

  • SSL/TLS encryption.
  • AES-256 encryption at rest (for contract-based data).
  • SOC 2 / ISO-compliant cloud infrastructure.
  • Access control and authentication.
  • Routine audits and vulnerability testing.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Data Breach Notification

If a breach occurs:

  • We will act to contain the incident.
  • Conduct internal investigations.
  • Notify affected users as required by law.

We are not liable for breaches beyond our reasonable control.

11. Your Rights

PDPA Rights

You may request:

  • Access to your personal data.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Withdrawal of consent.
  • Cessation of processing.

GDPR Rights

EU users may request:

  • Data access, deletion, restriction, or portability.
  • Objection to automated decision-making.
  • Filing a complaint with a supervisory authority.

CCPA Rights

California users may:

  • Request disclosure of collected data.
  • Request deletion.
  • Opt out of data selling (we do not sell personal data).

12. Cookies

We use cookies for functionality, analytics, and user experience improvement. Users may disable cookies in their browser settings.

13. Third-Party Links

14. Children’s Privacy

15. User Obligations and Indemnification

  • To ensure all uploaded information complies with PDPA and other regulations.
  • To indemnify RapidReportz for any claims arising from misuse, unlawful uploads, or violation of this policy.
